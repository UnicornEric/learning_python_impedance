Confirm the measured quantity/units, stimulation current/reference/pulse settings,
axis meanings, contact ordering, diagonal meaning, QC/compliance flags, technical
repeats, patient/implant/ear keys, visit timing, protocol changes, missingness, and
the separately measured scientific or predictive endpoint. The current bundle
is fictional V/I proxy data, not an assertion about MED-EL’s internal schema.

A matrix with 144 cells is still one visit; independence depends on the patient,
implant, and repeated-measurement structure. Match evaluation to unseen patients,
known-patient forecasting, or unseen sites before choosing a larger architecture.
